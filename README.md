## About

> "Engineering is all about perspective, and traveling is such a builder for it."

I build AI systems that have to survive production: LLM and retrieval services with evaluation gates, predictive and foundation models measured against real business baselines, and computer vision that runs in real time on real hardware. I have 5+ years of experience across machine learning, computer vision, and full-stack product engineering, and I still get disproportionately excited about a clean dataset, a stubborn edge case, and a well-shaped egg.

- **Senior AI Engineer** at Athene, based in West Des Moines, Iowa
- Previously led a five-engineer team (three offshore) as **Lead Software Engineer**, reporting to the CEO
- Building around **RAG**, **LLM gateways**, **MCP agents**, **foundation models**, and **computer vision**
- MS in Computer Science
- Lived in **7 countries**, visited **30+**

## What I work on

- **LLM systems.** Hybrid retrieval on Amazon Bedrock with recall-based deployment gates, LLM email triage and routing, and an AI coding gateway on LiteLLM and vLLM that routes between open-weight models and Claude.
- **Agents and tooling.** MCP servers and agent skills for Microsoft Graph, ServiceNow, Jira, and Jenkins, plus API contract governance with OpenAPI breaking-change checks in CI/CD.
- **Predictive and foundation models.** Gradient-boosted propensity models, a transformer foundation model pretrained on customer event sequences, and benchmarks against relational and time-series foundation models.
- **Computer vision and 3D perception.** Point-cloud detection, 3D head pose and gaze estimation, camera calibration, and ONNX/TensorRT deployment across Python services and C++ real-time runtimes.
- **Product engineering.** FastAPI, React, PostgreSQL, and Stripe platforms with payments, webhooks, and Stripe Connect payouts.
